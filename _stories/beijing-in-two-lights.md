The airport pictures came first, just as the light began to leave the runways. Two days later I followed Beijing from the old city into Olympic Park, staying out until the roads and towers had taken over the night.

{% include photo-strip.html id="approach" set=page.photo_sets.approach %}

At Capital Airport, Beijing still feels far away. Terminals sit low against the mountains, and each arriving aircraft holds the warm light for a few seconds before touching down.

{% include photo-strip.html id="old-city" set=page.photo_sets.old_city %}

In the old city, the view narrows to water, painted eaves and yellow leaves. From higher ground, the palace roofs open out again and run almost to the modern skyline.

{% include photo-strip.html id="olympic-park" set=page.photo_sets.olympic_park %}

By the time I reached Olympic Park, the last yellow leaves were still catching the daylight. Soon the tower, stadium and temporary installations were doing all the lighting themselves.

{% include photo-strip.html id="city-lines" set=page.photo_sets.city_lines %}

Farther into the city, I slowed the shutter and let the traffic draw through the frame. The roads joined buildings that otherwise felt isolated, turning the night into one continuous scene.
